# HumHub Mobile App

The official open source mobile app for [HumHub](https://www.humhub.com/).

This repository contains the Flutter app used to access HumHub networks on iPhone, iPad, and Android devices.

## What this App is for

The mobile app provides direct access to a HumHub network from a phone or tablet, so you can stay connected while away from a desktop browser. On top of everything you get from the browser, it adds a set of native capabilities:

### Push notifications
Get real-time native push notifications for activity in your network, whether the app is open, running in the background, or fully closed. Tapping a notification takes you straight to the relevant content inside the app instead of just opening the app to its home screen.

### Deep links
HumHub links opened anywhere on the device — in a browser, an email, a chat app, or another app — open directly inside the HumHub app instead of a mobile browser tab. If the device isn't configured to open those links in the app by default, the app will prompt you to enable that in your system settings.

### Share into HumHub
Share photos, videos, or files from any other app on your device — your camera roll, Files, a chat app — straight into HumHub. The shared content is uploaded automatically and opens HumHub's share dialog so you can post it to a space or send it as a message without switching apps first.

### Download, open, and share files
Download attachments and files from your network with a visible progress indicator, then choose what to do with them: open the file in another app, share it through your device's normal share sheet, or save it directly to your device's file system.

### Home screen quick actions
Long-press the app icon on your home screen to get shortcuts to your most recently used HumHub networks, so you can jump straight into a specific network without going through the network picker first.

### App lock
Optionally require Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint, or your device's PIN/passcode to open the app, adding an extra layer of privacy on top of your network's own login so HumHub content can't be viewed just by picking up an unlocked phone.

### Multi-network support
Connect to and store multiple HumHub networks on a single device. Switch between previously visited networks, add a new one at any time, or remove a saved network you no longer need.

### App icon badge
When your network sends push notifications, the app icon can show a badge for unread activity. The badge is cleared automatically as soon as you open the app.

### Offline detection
If your device loses internet access — or the HumHub server becomes unreachable — while you're using the app, including when resuming from the background, you'll see a clear offline notice with a retry option instead of a broken or blank screen.
